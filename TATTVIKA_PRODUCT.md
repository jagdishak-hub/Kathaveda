# Tattvika release direction

The project formerly presented as **The Reading Room / KathaVeda** is now being carried forward under the product name **Tattvika**.

This update preserves the mature codebase, source archives, persistent Railway storage and deployment history rather than replacing them with an earlier prototype.

## Existing mature capabilities retained

- 31 attributed literature collections
- 9,942 nonempty reading units
- complete named Sanskrit Shiva Purana edition with 457 chapters across seven samhitas
- historical Ramayana, Mahabharata and Vishnu Purana material
- Bhagavad Gita, Bhagavatam, Narayaneeyam, Rigveda, Narasimha Purana and additional source collections
- prepared retellings and separately labelled folktales
- Reading Room / source navigation
- Learn & chant with saved progress and scheduled review
- Converse with provider fallback
- scripture games with staged progression
- private study uploads
- persistent encrypted study profiles
- source coverage and rights reporting
- automated application and study tests

## Product principles going forward

1. **Database first.** The breadth, integrity and provenance of the literature library determine product quality.
2. **No false completeness.** Partial collections remain labelled partial.
3. **Read is canonical/source material.** AI prose is never silently substituted for scripture.
4. **Stories are separate.** Retellings and folktales are never presented as source text.
5. **Converse is evidence-grounded.** Answers should surface the actual literature basis and distinguish interpretation from source material.
6. **Games come from verified knowledge.** Question depth will expand with corpus coverage rather than relying on tiny sample banks.
7. **Offline and prepared paths matter.** Core reading and prepared material must remain usable when a provider is unavailable.
8. **BioSage-level product bar.** New releases should feel connected, persistent and substantial rather than like feature demos.

## Near-term Tattvika work

- carry the Tattvika name through the UI while keeping repository/deployment continuity
- broaden reader-first access to the full attributed library
- deepen cross-text Converse retrieval and comparison
- expand verified game banks and stage coverage
- add more reviewed modern explanations without confusing them with translations
- expand structured story/event coverage
- keep improving edition-level completeness checks and coverage reports
- improve review/memorization flows and pronunciation material

The public Railway service remains on the existing persistent deployment so study data and storage are not discarded during the rename.
